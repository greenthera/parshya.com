Parshya SEO Review

What the ten monthly SEO reports show, December 2025 to September 2026. Amounts in INR.

The month range applies to the numbers, charts and tables below. The findings and snapshot cards cover the whole period.
Our read

Ten months of steady link building and monthly content have not moved Google visibility. Impressions sit between 1,660 and 2,920 a month, and clicks have settled near 200. Organic search is a small channel, but it is the best traffic the site gets: about nine times more key events per session than paid or organic social. The larger problems are measurement and focus. Analytics conversion tracking is unreliable, most Analytics traffic is unattributed or barely engaged, and 69% of the 1,204 links went to bookmarking and classified-ad sites.

What the reports show

Whole period, December to September.

What is working

Organic search visitors are the best on the site.70.5% of organic search sessions were engaged, against 31.8% for paid social and 29.3% for unassigned traffic. Organic search produced 12 of the 33 key events recorded, from about 2% of sessions in those months.
Search clicks have recovered since July.Clicks rose from 148 in July to 221 in August (+49%) and 209 in September. Over the whole period, December to September is up 66%.
AI search visibility is growing from a small base.Semrush AI visibility went from 14 to 19 between April and June. Mentions doubled from 5 to 10 and cited pages rose from 1 to 3, all from ChatGPT. Google generative AI impressions grew from 225 in July to 352 in September (+56%).
The jumpsuit collection page is the first page-two ranking.Footer content was added to /collections/jumpsuit in July. The August and September reports show it at #13 for "designer jumpsuits" and #16 for "designer jumpsuit". These are the only keyword rankings reported.
Click-through rate holds near 10%.Seven of ten months were between 9.3% and 11.6%. At an average position of 6 to 8 this is typical of mostly branded searches, though the reports have no query data to confirm that.
Delivery was consistent.Every report lists 95 to 155 links, 1,204 in total, including 167 Pinterest pins and 140 articles. Seven blog posts, schema markup and competitor gap audits were also listed.

What is not working

Visibility is flat after ten months.Impressions were 2,250 in December and 2,260 in September. Clicks are 32% below the January peak of 309.
Average position has slipped.From 5.7 in May to 8.8 in September, with 10.5 in August, which is page two on average.
Organic search is a tiny channel.It is 0.7% to 4.9% of Analytics sessions, 285 to 567 sessions a month. Semrush shows 40 ranking keywords, a 0% traffic share and an Authority Score of 10. Both tracked keywords sit on page two.
Most link building uses low-value formats.Bookmarking (444) and classified ads (389) make up 69% of links, including auto-generated blog subdomains in February. These rarely move rankings and can carry risk if Google treats them as spam. The Authority Score of 10 after ten months fits that picture.
Promised work is not shown as done.Technical SEO checks (broken links, URL errors, duplicate or missing meta) were planned in the February and March reports. They never appear as completed and were dropped from the May plan. Product page meta tags were planned in every report and are never listed as completed. Meta tag work is not listed after June.
Analytics traffic is mostly noise.In April, 92.5% of 43,983 sessions were unassigned with 3 seconds of engagement. In June, 48% came from "organic video" at 2 seconds. August shows a 20-day surge that ends abruptly. The swing from 8.8k to 37.7k users is not SEO.
Conversion tracking is unreliable.Key events were recorded in three of eight months (January 27, February 3, June 3), with ₹5.24 lakh of revenue in total, ₹4.40 lakh of it in January. December, March, April, May and July recorded none despite 12k to 44k sessions, so SEO revenue cannot be measured.

What the reports do not show

No Search Console queries or pages.Without them there is no way to separate branded from non-branded search, or to see which pages earn the clicks.
Keyword rankings appear only twice.The August and September screenshots are identical, so no movement can be measured.
The Analytics section stops after July.August carries one overview card and September has none.
Audit results are never shown.The December keyword and backlink gap audits have no findings in any report. January lists them again as completed.
The summary text is repeated.It says one blog post was published in April and August, when none is listed. May and June list two.
No indexing, crawl, Core Web Vitals or backlink quality data.Semrush totals appear once, in September.

Suggested next steps

  1. Fix measurement first. Confirm the GA4 purchase event and revenue tracking, tag campaigns consistently to cut unassigned traffic, and filter low-engagement sources such as organic video and China or Singapore traffic.
  2. Report what Google shows. Add Search Console queries and pages, and track 25 to 30 non-brand keywords weekly with position history.
  3. Move effort from bookmarking to pages. Use the jumpsuit page as the template (footer content, FAQ, meta, schema) for wedding, cocktail and ready-to-ship collections, and publish about four non-brand blog posts a month.
  4. Replace mass links with a few good ones. Fashion blogs, press, influencer and marketplace features. Audit the existing link profile for spam.
  5. Finish and report the technical audit. Broken links, redirects, duplicate or missing meta, indexing and Core Web Vitals.
  6. Build on what converts. Organic search converts about nine times better per session than paid or organic social, so grow the pages that rank. Add FAQ and structured data to lift AI citations, which today come only from ChatGPT.
