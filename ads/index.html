Meta Ads lifetime report, 6 Sep 2023 to 6 Oct 2026. Amounts in INR. 50 campaigns, none currently delivering.

What is working

India WhatsApp retargeting brings the cheapest conversations.Six of the seven India WhatsApp campaigns cost ₹28 to ₹69 per conversation. "BOF- WA- IND- 11 May" alone produced 865 of the account's 1,963 conversations, at ₹34 each.
India top-of-funnel reach is very cheap.View-content campaigns in India cost ₹0.38 to ₹0.58 per result (90,242 results for ₹43,633). Profile visits cost ₹0.38 to ₹0.77. These build audiences to retarget, though this export cannot show how many turned into sales.
Eoss-10Jan delivered the most purchases.9 purchases at ₹9,269 each, against an average of ₹14,395 across all purchase campaigns. The other four purchase campaigns recorded 1 or 2 purchases each.

Where money was lost

A quarter of all spend recorded no result.15 campaigns spent ₹1,95,478 (25.5%) with nothing logged. Ten of them are purchase campaigns (₹97,474). Check that the purchase event was firing before blaming the ads.
Purchases are expensive.16 recorded purchases cost ₹2,30,319, or ₹14,395 each. "TOF-Tier1-Cities-9Dec" spent ₹85,010 for 2 purchases (₹42,505 each). Whether this is profitable depends on order value, which the export does not include.
USA WhatsApp costs 8 times the India price.₹93,442 bought 266 conversations at ₹351 each. "TOF- WhatsApp- USA" alone spent ₹75,079 for 250 conversations (₹300 each). No USA campaign recorded a purchase.

Suggested next steps

  • Put new budget behind India WhatsApp retargeting, fed by the cheap view-content and profile-visit campaigns.
  • Confirm the purchase event is tracked correctly before relaunching any purchase campaign. Ten of them logged zero results.
  • Keep USA WhatsApp paused unless a USA order is worth well over ₹350 per conversation, or retest it with the creative that works in India.
  • Add conversion value (revenue) to the next export so return on ad spend can be calculated.
